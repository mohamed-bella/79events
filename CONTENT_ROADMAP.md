## Evidence needed before publication

- Location details grounded in current venue, transport and supplier checks.
- Venue capacities, facilities, accessibility, certifications, pricing and availability with a source and verification date.
- Client approval for names, logos, photographs, testimonials and case-study outcomes.
- Confirmed company description, contact details, legal policies, team biographies and statistics.
- Original French translation and unique metadata for each translated route.
- A distinct search purpose and cross-link plan for each new indexable page.
